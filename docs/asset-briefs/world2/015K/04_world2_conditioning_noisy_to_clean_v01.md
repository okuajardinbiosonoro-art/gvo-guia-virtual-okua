Show signal conditioning as transformation: noisy input, filter/conditioning module, clean output. This replaces the decorative energy field as the primary layer 4 semantic visual.

## Prompt

Create a transparent PNG visualizing signal conditioning for a plant bioelectric interface. On the left, show a noisy low-amplitude waveform. In the center, show a minimal filter/conditioning block made of elegant abstract circuitry. On the right, show a cleaner, amplified waveform. Use restrained cyan, gold, and violet glow. The visual must be clear on mobile and must not look like fantasy magic. No text, no background, no floral mandala.
