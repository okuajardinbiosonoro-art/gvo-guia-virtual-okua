Create a transparent PNG of a weak noisy bioelectric waveform before signal conditioning. The line should be thin, irregular, low amplitude in some segments, with visible noise and jitter. Keep it technical and measurement-like, using restrained cyan, pale violet, and small warm signal points. No text, no background, no decorative plant branches, no fantasy energy cloud.
