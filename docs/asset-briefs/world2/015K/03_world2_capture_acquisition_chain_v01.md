## Function

Show the acquisition chain: plant contact/electrode, input module, and data entering the system. This replaces the abstract capture field as the primary layer 3 semantic visual.

## Prompt

Create a transparent PNG for a poetic-scientific plant signal acquisition chain. Show a small contact point on a plant stem or leaf edge, a fine electrode/sensor line, a compact microcontroller/input module silhouette, and small data pulses entering the module. The composition must be readable on mobile, elegant, and non-photorealistic. Use cyan/gold measurement accents with restrained violet glow. No text, no background, no oversized UI, no magical energy burst.

Animate contact pulse, then data beads moving along the line into the module. Keep module static and signal motion subtle.
