## Canvas Choice

Use `1400x1400` because the contact node must be positionable over plant details as a compact overlay. The square canvas supports reuse as a local node, hover/focus marker, or sensor anchor.

## Function

Make visible the point where the plant is read by the system. This asset links plant presence to signal without implying direct music.

## Prompt

Create a transparent PNG compact bioelectric contact node for a plant interface. Show a subtle sensor/electrode contact point that can sit on a plant stem or leaf, with a small technical ring, tiny signal particles, and one clean outgoing signal line stub. Use cyan and warm gold accents with very restrained violet glow. The asset must be readable at small sizes and must not cover the plant. No text, no background, no large magical aura.
